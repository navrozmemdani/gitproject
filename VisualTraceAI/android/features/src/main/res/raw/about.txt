# VisualTrace AI
Version {version}

Discover anything visually. VisualTrace AI finds visually similar images across publicly accessible websites and supported platforms.

## How it works
- Your image is analysed (dimensions, colours, quality, objects and visible text where available).
- Perceptual hashes and a visual embedding are computed.
- Configured providers are searched through official or licensed APIs.
- Candidate images are compared visually, deduplicated and ranked.

## Honest by design
- Every result shows which provider found it and how each field was obtained: from the source, measured, inferred, or not available.
- Providers that are not configured are listed as such; the app never fabricates results.

## Open-source components
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Hilt, Retrofit, OkHttp, Room, WorkManager, DataStore, Coil, Media3, Firebase Authentication; backend: FastAPI, PostgreSQL, OpenCV, OpenCLIP.
