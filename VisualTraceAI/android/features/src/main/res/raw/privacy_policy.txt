# Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 2026

VisualTrace AI ("we") helps you find visually similar images on public websites. This policy explains what data the app and the VisualTrace backend process, and the controls you have.

## What we process
- Images and video frames you choose to search. Video frames are extracted on your device; the full video is uploaded only if you explicitly enable it.
- Before upload, images are re-encoded on your device, which removes embedded metadata such as GPS location.
- Account data (email, display name) when you sign in with email or Google through Firebase Authentication.
- A random guest identifier when you continue as a guest.
- Search history, results and saved items.

## Retention
- Uploaded images, frames and videos are stored in temporary storage only while a search is processed and are deleted automatically afterwards (at most 15 minutes after upload, even if processing fails).
- Search history is stored on your device by default. A server copy of each search (results and a small preview thumbnail, if history is enabled) is kept so you can reopen it; you can delete individual searches or all history at any time, and deletions are applied on the server too.
- Saved results are stored on your device. They are copied to your account only if you enable Cloud sync.

## Third-party search providers
- Keyword-based providers (e.g. Google Programmable Search, Reddit, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons) receive only text keywords derived from the image analysis or your description.
- Upload-based reverse-image providers (e.g. TinEye, Google Lens via SerpApi) receive a copy of your image only if you consent. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy.
- We only use official APIs and authorised search services. We never bypass logins, private accounts or CAPTCHAs.

## What we never do
- We do not perform facial recognition or try to identify people.
- We do not present similarity as proof that two images show the same person, share an owner or have the same origin.
- We do not sell personal data.

## Your controls
- Delete individual searches or clear all history (Settings or History screen).
- Delete all of your server-side data (Profile > Delete my server data).
- Turn history saving and cloud sync on or off.
- Withdraw consent for upload-based providers at any time.

## Security
All traffic uses HTTPS. Provider API keys are stored only on the server. Uploads are validated, size-limited and optionally scanned for malware.

## Contact
Questions or requests: use "Contact support" in Settings.
